> Summary for guidance only. It does not replace first-aid training or the instructions given by 112.

## 1. Protect
- Check that **you** are not in danger (traffic, fire, electricity, collapse, water). Don't become another casualty.
- If it is safe, move the casualty away from the danger only if they cannot stay where they are.

## 2. Alert
- Call **112** (the single emergency number in the EU).
- Say **what** has happened, **where** (GPS coordinates if you have them), **how many** casualties there are and their condition (are they responding? are they breathing?).
- Don't hang up until you are told to. Put the phone on speaker so your hands are free.

## 3. Help
- Are they responsive? Speak to them loudly and tap their shoulders.
- Are they breathing normally? Look, listen and feel for no more than 10 seconds.
- **Unresponsive and not breathing normally** → CPR ("Adult CPR" protocol).
- **Unresponsive but breathing** → recovery position and monitor their breathing.
- Severe bleeding → immediate direct pressure ("Severe bleeding" protocol).

## Warnings
- Don't give food or drink to a person with an altered level of consciousness.
- Don't move a trauma casualty unless there is imminent danger.
