> Summary for guidance only, based on the ERC 2021 guidelines. Hands-on training is essential.

## When
The person is **unresponsive** and **not breathing normally** (gasping is not normal breathing).

## Steps
1. Call **112** (on speaker) and ask for an **AED** (defibrillator) if there is one nearby.
2. Lay the casualty on their back on a firm surface.
3. Place the heel of one hand on the **centre of the chest**, the other hand on top, with your arms straight.
4. Compress to a depth of **5–6 cm** at **100–120 compressions per minute**. Let the chest rise fully between compressions.
5. If you know how and are willing: **30 compressions and 2 rescue breaths**. If not, continuous compressions only.
6. As soon as the AED arrives: switch it on and follow its voice prompts.
7. Don't stop until professional help arrives, the casualty breathes normally or you become exhausted. If someone else is there, take turns every 2 minutes.

## Warnings
- Keep interruptions to compressions to a minimum.
- Ribs may break; that is not a reason to stop.
