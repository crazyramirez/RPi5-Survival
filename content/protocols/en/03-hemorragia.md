> Summary for guidance only. Severe bleeding can be fatal within minutes: act fast.

## Steps
1. Protect yourself if possible (gloves or a plastic bag).
2. Call **112**.
3. Apply **firm direct pressure** to the wound with a dressing, gauze or clean cloth, continuously.
4. If blood soaks through the dressing, **don't remove it**: add more on top and keep pressing.
5. Life-threatening bleeding **from an arm or a leg** that is not controlled by direct pressure: apply a **tourniquet** 5–7 cm above the wound (never over a joint) and tighten it until the bleeding stops. **Write down the time** it was applied.
6. Keep the casualty warm and monitor their consciousness and breathing.

## Warnings
- Don't remove a tourniquet once it has been applied: healthcare staff will do it.
- Don't pull out embedded objects; press around them and immobilise them.
