> Summary for guidance only, based on the ERC 2021 guidelines.

## If they can cough
- Encourage them to **keep coughing**. Do nothing else and keep watching them.

## If they cannot cough, speak or breathe properly
1. Lean them forward and give up to **5 back blows**, firmly, between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
2. If that doesn't clear it: up to **5 abdominal thrusts** (Heimlich manoeuvre). From behind, place your fist between the navel and the breastbone and pull inwards and upwards.
3. Alternate 5 blows and 5 thrusts until the obstruction clears or they lose consciousness.
4. If they lose consciousness: call **112** and start **CPR**.

## Warnings
- After abdominal thrusts, the person must be assessed by a doctor even if they recover.
- In pregnant women and people with obesity: chest thrusts instead of abdominal thrusts.
