> Summary for guidance only.

## Steps
1. Move the person away from the heat source.
2. **Cool with clean water** at room temperature or cool water for **at least 20 minutes**, as soon as possible.
3. Remove rings, watches and any clothing that is not stuck to the skin before the area swells.
4. Cover the burn with **cling film** (loosely) or with a clean non-adherent dressing.
5. Call **112** if the burn is large or deep, if it affects the face, hands, genitals or airway, or if it is electrical or chemical.

## Warnings
- **Don't** use ice, toothpaste, oils or home remedies.
- Don't burst blisters.
- Watch for hypothermia when cooling large burns, especially in children.
