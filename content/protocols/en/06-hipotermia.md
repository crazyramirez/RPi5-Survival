> Summary for guidance only. Severe hypothermia is a medical emergency.

## Signs
Intense shivering, clumsiness, confusion and drowsiness. In severe cases the shivering **stops**: this is a worse sign, not a better one.

## Steps
1. Call **112** if there is confusion, drowsiness or loss of consciousness.
2. Take the person to a place sheltered from the wind, the rain and the cold ground.
3. Remove **wet** clothing, replace it with dry clothing and insulate them from the ground.
4. Cover them with blankets, a sleeping bag or a foil (thermal) blanket, including the head.
5. If they are **conscious and able to swallow**: warm, sugary drinks (never alcohol).
6. Handle them gently and keep them horizontal if the hypothermia is severe.

## Warnings
- **Don't** rub the limbs or apply intense direct heat.
- If they are not breathing normally: CPR. Hypothermia can protect the brain; don't give up resuscitation early.
