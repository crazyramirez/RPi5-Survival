> Summary for guidance only. Heatstroke is potentially fatal.

## Signs
Very high body temperature, hot skin, confusion, dizziness, nausea, seizures or loss of consciousness.

## Steps
1. Call **112**.
2. Move the person into the shade or to a cool place.
3. **Cool them immediately**. The most effective method is to **immerse the body** (from the neck down) in cold water. If that is not possible, soak them with water and fan them, and apply cold compresses to the neck, armpits and groin.
4. If they are conscious and able to swallow: water or a drink with salts in small sips.
5. If they lose consciousness and are not breathing normally: CPR.

## Warnings
- Don't delay cooling while waiting for the ambulance.
- Don't give them fever medication: it is not effective in heatstroke.
