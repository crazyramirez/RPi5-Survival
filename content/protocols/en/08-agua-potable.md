> Summary for guidance only. No method removes **chemical** contaminants (fuels, metals, pesticides): if you suspect chemical contamination, find another source.

## Before disinfecting
- If the water is **cloudy**, let it settle and filter it through a clean cloth or a coffee filter. The methods below work less well with cloudy water.

## Boiling (preferred method)
- Bring the water to a **rolling boil for 1 minute**.
- Above **2000 m altitude**: **3 minutes**.
- Let it cool covered and store it in clean, closed containers.

## Bleach (chlorine)
- Use bleach that is **suitable for disinfecting drinking water**, with no perfume or additives (the label says so).
- With bleach of **~5–6 %** chlorine: **2 drops per litre** of clear water (4 if it is cloudy or cold).
- Mix and wait **30 minutes**. There should be a slight smell of chlorine; if not, repeat the dose and wait another 15 minutes.

## Solar disinfection (SODIS)
- Use **clear PET** bottles of up to 2 litres, clean and unscratched, filled with clear water.
- Leave them in direct sunlight for **6 hours**. If the sky is cloudy more than half the time, **2 consecutive days**.
- It does not work during continuous rain or with cloudy water.

## Warnings
- Store treated water in clean containers with lids, and pour it out without putting hands or cups into it.
