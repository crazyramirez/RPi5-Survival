> Summary for guidance only. In a stroke every minute counts: treatment depends on getting to hospital quickly.

## Signs (they appear suddenly)
- **Face**: one side of the mouth droops when they smile.
- **Arm**: they cannot raise both arms, or one drifts down.
- **Speech**: strange speech, slurred words or not understanding what is said to them.
- Also: sudden loss of vision, a very severe and sudden headache, dizziness or loss of balance for no reason.

## Steps
1. If **any** of these signs appears, call **112** immediately and say you suspect a **stroke**.
2. **Write down the time** the symptoms started or the last time they were seen well.
3. Keep the person calm, sitting or lying in a comfortable position, and loosen their clothing.
4. If they are unresponsive but breathing: **recovery position**. If they are not breathing normally: **CPR**.
5. Gather their usual medication or their list of medicines to hand over to the healthcare staff.
6. Don't leave them alone, and monitor their consciousness and breathing until help arrives.

## Warnings
- **Don't** give them food, drink or medicines (not aspirin either): they may choke, and some strokes are bleeds.
- **Don't** wait to see if it passes: even if the symptoms disappear, call anyway.
- If possible, don't take them yourself: 112 activates the "Código Ictus" (stroke code) and takes them to the appropriate hospital.
