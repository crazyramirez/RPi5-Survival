> Guidance summary. Most seizures stop on their own within a few minutes.

## Steps
1. **Stay calm** and **check the time**: time the seizure.
2. **Move away** dangerous objects and protect their head with something soft (a folded jacket).
3. Loosen clothing around their neck and remove their glasses.
4. **Do not hold them down** or try to stop the movements.
5. When the movements stop, place them in the **recovery position** and check that they are **breathing normally**.
6. Stay with them until they are fully awake; it is normal for them to be confused for a while.
7. Call **112** if the seizure lasts **5 minutes or more**, if it recurs without recovery between seizures, if it is the **first time**, if they have been injured, if it happened in **water**, if they are pregnant or have diabetes, or if they do not regain consciousness or are struggling to breathe.

## Warnings
- **Do not** put anything in their mouth (no fingers, objects or liquids): they will not "swallow their tongue".
- Do not give them anything to eat or drink until they have fully recovered.
- If afterwards they are **not breathing normally**: CPR. A cardiac arrest can begin with movements that look like a seizure.
