> Guidance summary. Shock is a failure of the circulation (due to bleeding, severe allergy, infection or a heart problem) and requires urgent care.

## Signs
**Pale, cold and sweaty** skin, very fast or very slow pulse, rapid breathing, dizziness, thirst, confusion or drowsiness.

## Steps
1. Call **112**.
2. Treat the cause if you know it: **bleeding** → direct pressure; **anaphylaxis** → adrenaline auto-injector.
3. **Lay them on their back**.
4. If there is no injury to the legs, hips, head or spine, you can **raise their legs** passively while you wait.
5. **Cover them** from above and insulate them from the ground to stop them losing heat.
6. Reassure them, do not leave them alone and **monitor** their consciousness and breathing.
7. If they stop responding but are breathing: **recovery position** (if there is no trauma). If they are not breathing normally: **CPR**.

## Warnings
- **Do not** give them anything to eat or drink.
- Do not get them up or make them walk.
- If lying them down makes breathing harder, leave them half-sitting.
