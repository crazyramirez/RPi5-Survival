> Guidance summary. After a blow to the head, signs of a serious injury can appear hours later.

## Steps
1. If they **do not respond**: call **112**. If they are breathing, **do not move them** unless there is danger (there may be a spinal injury). If they are not breathing normally: **CPR**.
2. If they are conscious, they must **immediately stop** all physical or sporting activity.
3. If the scalp is bleeding, **press** with a clean dressing. If you feel a dent in the bone, press around it, not on it.
4. Apply **cold** wrapped in a cloth to the bump.
5. Call **112** if there is: loss of consciousness (even brief), **repeated vomiting**, a headache that **gets worse**, drowsiness or confusion, **seizures**, pupils of different sizes, weakness or tingling in the arms or legs, fluid or blood coming from the nose or ears, or if they take **anticoagulants**.
6. Even if it seems minor, have them assessed by a health professional and make sure **someone stays with them** for at least the first 24 hours.

## Warnings
- Do not give them alcohol or sedatives.
- They must not return to sport until a health professional allows it.
