> Guidance summary. For heavy bleeding use the "Severe bleeding" protocol.

## Steps
1. Wash your hands and put on gloves if you have them.
2. If it is bleeding, **press** with a clean dressing or cloth until it stops. If the bleeding is heavy or does not stop, call **112** ("Severe bleeding" protocol).
3. **Wash the wound with plenty of running drinking water**, with soap around it, until visible dirt is removed.
4. Carefully remove surface debris; **do not pull out** embedded objects.
5. Cover with a clean dressing. Change it if it gets wet or dirty.
6. Seek medical care if it is **deep**, wide or gaping, if it is on the face, hands, joints or genitals, if it is a **bite**, if it is very dirty (soil, rust) or if you are not sure about your **tetanus vaccination**.
7. **Amputation**: control the bleeding, wrap the amputated part in moistened clean gauze or cloth, put it in a sealed bag and place that bag inside another containing **water and ice**. Call **112**, note the time, and make sure it travels with the casualty.
8. Over the following days watch for signs of **infection**: spreading redness, warmth, swelling, pus, red streaks or fever. If they appear, seek urgent medical advice.

## Warnings
- **Do not** put the amputated part in direct contact with ice or freeze it.
- Do not put home remedies (soil, herbs, coffee, toothpaste) on the wound: clean water is enough to wash it.
