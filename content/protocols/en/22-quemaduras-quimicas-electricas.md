> Guidance summary. Protect yourself first: do not touch the product or a casualty who is still in contact with the electric current.

## Chemical burn
1. Put on gloves if you can. If the product is a **powder**, brush it off before wetting.
2. Remove contaminated clothing, jewellery and footwear.
3. **Wash with plenty of water** under a gentle stream for **at least 15 minutes**, longer if it still hurts.
4. **Eyes**: rinse them immediately with plenty of clean, continuously running water for **at least 15 minutes**, with the eyelids open and the affected eye facing down so as not to contaminate the other. Remove contact lenses if they come out easily.
5. Call **112** or the **Servicio de Información Toxicológica (Spanish poison information service) (91 562 04 20)** and keep the product's container.

## Electrical burn
1. **Switch off the power** (fuse box, unplug) before touching the casualty. If you cannot, separate them with a dry, non-conducting object (wood, plastic).
2. **High voltage** (power lines, overhead railway lines, substations): do not go near; call **112**.
3. If they do not respond and are not breathing normally: **CPR** and **AED**.
4. Cool visible burns with water and cover them with a clean dressing.
5. Call **112** even if it seems minor: internal injuries and heart rhythm disturbances may not be visible.

## Warnings
- **Do not** try to neutralise the product with another one (vinegar, bicarbonate of soda).
- Electrical burns usually have an entry point and an exit point: look for both.
