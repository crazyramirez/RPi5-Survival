> Guidance summary of the ERC 2025 guidelines. Many people die attempting a rescue without training: do not become another victim.

## Rescue
1. Shout for help and have someone call **112**.
2. **Do not enter the water** if you have no lifesaving training. Help from land: throw them a **floating object** (lifebuoy, empty jerrycan, cool box) or reach out with a pole, a branch or a rope.
3. If you are a lifeguard and go in: take a flotation device and keep their head out of the water.

## Out of the water
1. If they **do not respond and are not breathing** normally: give **5 rescue breaths** and start **CPR** (30 compressions and 2 breaths).
2. **Dry the chest** and attach the **AED** as soon as it is available.
3. If they are breathing: recovery position, remove their wet clothing and keep them warm.

## Warnings
- **Do not** try to get water out of the lungs (no abdominal thrusts, no turning them face down): it delays resuscitation.
- If they vomit during CPR, turn them on their side, clear the mouth and continue.
- Even if they recover, they must be assessed by a doctor: breathing problems can appear hours later.
- If they dived into shallow water, suspect a spinal injury.
