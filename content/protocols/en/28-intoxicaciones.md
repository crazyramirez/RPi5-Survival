> Guidance summary. Servicio de Información Toxicológica (Spanish poison information service, 24 hours): **91 562 04 20**. If there are severe symptoms, call **112** first.

## Steps
1. Move the person away from the product and remove whatever is left in their mouth.
2. If they **do not respond, are struggling to breathe or are having a seizure**: call **112**. If they are not breathing normally, **CPR** (do compressions only if the poison could contaminate you).
3. If they feel well, call the **Servicio de Información Toxicológica (91 562 04 20)** and follow their instructions.
4. Have to hand the **container or label** (or remains of mushrooms, plants or pills), the approximate **amount**, the **time**, and the person's age and weight.
5. **Skin**: remove contaminated clothing and wash with plenty of water. **Eyes**: rinse them with water for at least 15 minutes.
6. **Gases or fumes**: get out into the fresh air ("Carbon monoxide poisoning" protocol).
7. **Mushrooms**: always seek advice, even if there are no symptoms yet: with the most dangerous mushrooms they can take hours to appear.
8. **Opioids** (very slow breathing, very small pupils, will not wake up): call **112**, start CPR if they are not breathing normally and use **naloxone** if available.

## Warnings
- **Do not induce vomiting** or give milk, water, oil or other remedies unless a health professional tells you to.
- With caustics (bleach, drain cleaners, ammonia): rinse the mouth and do not make them vomit.
- Keep cleaning products and medicines out of the reach of children and in their original container.
