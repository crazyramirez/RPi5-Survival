> Summary for guidance only, based on the ERC 2025 guidelines. The viper is the only highly venomous native snake in Europe. Any snake bite requires hospital assessment.

## Signs
Two fang marks, intense pain, swelling and bruising that spread; sometimes nausea, dizziness or general malaise.

## Steps
1. Move away from the snake; **do not try to catch it** or kill it. If you can, take a photo of it from a distance.
2. Call **112**.
3. Keep the person **calm and at rest**, lying down or sitting.
4. **Immobilise** the bitten limb, as if it were a fracture, and keep it still.
5. Remove **rings, watches and tight clothing** before it swells.
6. The person should **not walk** if it can be avoided. Wait for the ambulance or follow the instructions from 112.
7. Write down the time of the bite and how the symptoms develop.

## Warnings (do not)
- **Do not** apply a tourniquet or a compression bandage.
- **Do not** apply ice or heat.
- **Do not** cut the wound or try to **suck out** the venom.
- Do not give alcohol or medicines on your own initiative.
