> Summary for guidance only. Smoke kills before flames do: get out as soon as possible and do not go back in.

## Steps
1. Shout to warn everyone and **get out immediately**. Do not stop to pick up belongings.
2. Call **112** as soon as you are outside.
3. If there is smoke, **move crouching or on all fours**, close to the floor, and cover your mouth and nose with a cloth, preferably damp.
4. Before opening a door, **touch it with the back of your hand**: if it is hot, do not open it and look for another way out.
5. **Close the doors** behind you to slow the fire and smoke.
6. **Do not use the lift**; go down the stairs. If the stairway is full of smoke, do not use it.
7. If you cannot get out: shut yourself in a room with a window, **seal the gaps** around the door with wet towels or clothing and **make yourself seen** from the window. Tell 112 where you are.
8. If your clothes catch fire: **stop, drop and roll**, covering your face. Then cool the burns with water.

## Warnings
- Use an extinguisher only if the fire is **small** and the way out is behind you.
- **Do not** throw water on an oil fire or an electrical fire: cover the pan with a lid and switch off the electricity.
- Outside, gather at an agreed meeting point and **do not go back in** for anything or anyone: tell the fire brigade.
