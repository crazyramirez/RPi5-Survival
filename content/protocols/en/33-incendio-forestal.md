> Summary for guidance only. Always follow the authorities' evacuation or shelter-in-place orders: leaving in time is the safest option.

## If you see fire or smoke
1. Call **112**: say where you are (GPS coordinates) and what you can see.
2. **Move away** in the direction opposite to the fire's advance and the wind, towards areas **without vegetation**, roads, rivers or already burnt areas.
3. **Do not flee uphill** or through ravines: fire climbs slopes very fast.
4. By car: turn on your lights, drive slowly and **do not drive through the fire or dense smoke**.
5. If the fire reaches you in the car: stop in a clear area, close the windows and ventilation and **stay inside**, crouched below the windows and covered with a blanket, until the fire front has passed.
6. If you are surrounded on foot: look for an **already burnt area** or one without fuel. Wet your clothes and cover your mouth and nose with a damp cloth.
7. As a last resort: lie down in a ditch, a hollow or behind a rock, with your face covered.

## If you are at home and have not been evacuated
1. Close doors and windows and move curtains and flammable objects away from them.
2. Fill bathtubs and buckets with water; wet the surroundings if possible.
3. Have the emergency backpack ready and the car facing the exit. Listen to the radio and to **ES-Alert** warnings on your mobile phone.

## Warnings
- Do not light fires or barbecues in the countryside and do not throw away cigarette ends or glass.
- Do not return to the area until it is authorised: the fire can flare up again.
