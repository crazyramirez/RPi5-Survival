> Summary for guidance only. Most injuries are caused by falling objects and by trying to run out during the shaking.

## During the shaking
1. **Drop, cover and hold on**: get down on your knees, take cover under a sturdy table (or next to an interior wall, covering your head and neck with your arms) and hold on until it stops.
2. **Stay away from windows**, mirrors, shelves, lamps and building façades.
3. If you are **inside, stay inside**: do not run for the exit or use the lift or the stairs while it is shaking.
4. If you are **outside**: move away from buildings, poles, trees and power lines.
5. **Driving**: stop in a clear place, away from bridges, tunnels and embankments, and stay in the car.
6. On the **coast**, if the shaking is strong or long or the sea recedes abnormally: go **immediately to high ground** or inland, without waiting for warnings (risk of **tsunami**).

## Afterwards
1. Expect **aftershocks**. Check for injured people and give first aid. Call **112** if there are seriously injured or trapped people.
2. If you smell gas, **do not switch on** anything: close the shut-off valve and get out ("Gas leak" protocol).
3. Leave wearing shoes and with care if the building is damaged; do not use the lift.
4. Use the phone only for emergencies and get information from the radio and official channels.

## Warnings
- Do not shelter under a door frame: in modern homes it gives no more protection than other areas.
- Do not return to a damaged building until it has been checked by experts.
