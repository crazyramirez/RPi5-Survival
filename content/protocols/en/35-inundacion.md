> Summary for guidance only. Many flood deaths happen when trying to cross by car or when going down to garages and basements: seek **height**, not your car.

## When a warning is issued
1. Follow the warnings from **AEMET** and Protección Civil and the **ES-Alert** alerts on your mobile phone.
2. Take documents, valuables and the emergency backpack up to the **upper floors**.
3. Get ready to evacuate and follow the authorities' orders.

## During the flood
1. Go up to a **high floor** or onto the roof. **Do not go down to garages, basements** or ground floors, not even to get the car out.
2. **Switch off the electricity and gas** if you can do so without stepping in water.
3. **Do not cross** flooded streets, fords or bridges, either on foot or by car: about 15 cm of moving water can knock you over and about 30 cm can sweep away most cars.
4. If the car becomes trapped and the water is rising, **get out of it** and find high ground.
5. Stay away from watercourses, ravines and seasonal riverbeds (ramblas), even if they are dry.
6. Call **112** if you are in danger and say exactly where you are.

## Afterwards
- Do not drink tap water until it is authorised; if in doubt, purify it ("Making water safe to drink" protocol).
- Avoid contact with floodwater (it may be contaminated or electrified) and do not use electrical appliances that have got wet.
