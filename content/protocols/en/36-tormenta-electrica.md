> Summary for guidance only. No place outdoors is safe during a thunderstorm.

## Steps
1. If you **hear thunder**, you are already within reach of lightning: **seek shelter immediately**, without waiting for it to rain.
2. Shelter in a **building** or in a **car** with a metal roof and the windows closed.
3. Stay away from **isolated trees**, summits, ridges, towers, poles and metal fences.
4. Get out of the **water** (beach, swimming pool, river) and move away from the shore.
5. Let go of long metal objects (walking poles, umbrellas, tools) and get off your bicycle or motorbike.
6. In a group outdoors, **spread out** a few metres apart from each other.
7. If there is no shelter: crouch in a low area with your **feet together** and your head down, without lying on the ground. It is a last resort: it does not fully protect you.
8. Wait **at least 30 minutes** after the last thunder before going out.

## If someone is struck by lightning
- You can **touch them**: no electricity remains in the body. Call **112**.
- If they are not breathing normally: **CPR** and **AED**. If there are several victims, attend first to those who **are not breathing**.

## Warnings
- Indoors, avoid the shower, taps and plugged-in corded appliances during the storm.
- Do not shelter in small caves or under rock overhangs.
