> Summary for guidance only. Household gas has an added smell (of rotten eggs or "of gas") so that leaks can be detected.

## Steps
1. **Do not switch** lights or electrical appliances **on or off**, do not touch doorbells or plugs and **do not make any flames** (matches, lighters, tobacco).
2. **Open doors and windows** to ventilate.
3. **Close the gas shut-off valve** or the valve on the butane cylinder.
4. **Leave the home** with all people and animals.
5. **Call from outside** to **112** or to your gas distributor's emergency number. Do not use the phone inside.
6. Warn the neighbours by knocking on their door with your knuckles, not with the doorbell.
7. Do not go back in until the fire brigade or a technician authorises it.

## Warnings
- **Do not** use the lift or the door entry phone.
- If someone is dizzy or unconscious, take them out into the open air only if you can do so without risk, and call 112.
- If there is already a fire, do not try to close a burning cylinder: get out and call 112.
