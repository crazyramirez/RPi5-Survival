> Summary for guidance only. In extreme cold the most vulnerable are older people, babies, people without a home and the chronically ill.

## At home
1. Heat **a single room** and gather in it; close the others and block the gaps.
2. Wear **several layers** of loose clothing, a hat and socks; use blankets.
3. Eat hot food and drink fluids; avoid alcohol.
4. **Never** use braziers, outdoor heaters, barbecues or generators inside the home. If you use butane heaters, ventilate and turn them off when sleeping.
5. Visit or call **elderly neighbours** or those who live alone.

## Outdoors or without shelter
1. Find **shelter from the wind** and damp and **insulate yourself from the ground** with branches, your backpack or cardboard.
2. **Stay dry**: change out of wet clothes and avoid sweating (remove layers before exerting yourself).
3. Cover your **head, neck, hands and feet**. In a group, stay together to share heat.
4. **Trapped in the car by snow**: stay inside; run the engine only now and then, with the **exhaust pipe clear of snow** and a window slightly open (risk of carbon monoxide).
5. Watch for signs of **hypothermia** (intense shivering, clumsiness, confusion) and of **frostbite** (white, numb skin) and apply their protocols.

## Warnings
- Alcohol gives a feeling of warmth but **promotes hypothermia**.
- Call **112** if someone is confused, drowsy or unresponsive.
